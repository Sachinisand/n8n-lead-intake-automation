Routing rules
  • Required: name, message and a valid email (email is lower-cased).
  • Sector from keywords: hotel, restaurant → hospitality · Lager, warehouse → logistics · Klinik, Pflege → healthcare · Supermarkt → retail.
  • Use case: Reinigung / cleaning · Serviceroboter / serving · Transport / Lieferung.
  • Priority: high = asks for a quote or price and has 50+ employees · medium = one of the two · normal = neither.
  • Reply time: high 4 h · medium 24 h · normal 48 h.
